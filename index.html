    A world in every cup

    Beanscape is a reference for people who drink coffee and want to understand it. Where it came from, why a cortado is not a flat white, what roasting actually changes, and how the same bean becomes six different drinks in six different countries.

    Nothing here is for sale. It is a self-initiated project, written and built in the open.
